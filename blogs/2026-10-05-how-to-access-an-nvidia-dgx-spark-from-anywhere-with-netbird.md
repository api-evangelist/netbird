Put your DGX Spark on a NetBird network and SSH into it from any network without opening a single port. We install the NetBird CLI on DGX OS, enroll the Spark with a setup key, limit access to SSH from your own devices, and reach notebooks and model servers on the Spark through SSH port forwarding.
